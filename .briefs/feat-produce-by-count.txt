# BRIEF: Large produce should be counted, not weighed

## Context

**The Good Pantry.** Owner, from real use:

> *"Bigger fruits and vegetables need to be quantised per fruit/vegetable, not lbs or grams.
> That would really only apply to like blueberries or grapes — smaller fruits."*

Correct. "1.984 lb of apples" is not how anyone thinks about their kitchen. "4 apples" is. But
berries genuinely are a weight, so this is a per-ingredient judgement, not a blanket rule.

Read `SPEC.md` (the units section — **form** is a first-class axis) and
`packages/core/src/seed/`.

## Scope

`packages/core/src/seed/**` and its tests. You may add conversion edges and adjust
`defaultFormId` / `dim` on produce.

**Do NOT touch** `packages/core/src/units/**`, `src/pantry/**`, `src/recipes/**` (280 tests —
the conversion machinery is correct and already supports this), `apps/web/**`, `supabase/**`,
or `native/**`.

## 1. Classify produce by how people actually handle it

**Count** — things you pick up individually: apples, oranges, bananas, lemons, limes, avocados,
peppers, onions, potatoes, sweet potatoes, cucumbers, zucchini, eggplant, tomatoes (standard,
not cherry), peaches, pears, mangoes, and heads of lettuce / cabbage / broccoli / cauliflower.

**Weight or volume** — things you scoop or pour: blueberries, raspberries, blackberries,
strawberries, grapes, cherry tomatoes, peas, green beans, corn kernels, salad greens, spinach.

**Bunch** — where a bunch is the natural unit: herbs, bananas (arguably), celery, kale, chard.
Keep `bunch` as a form with a **high `uncertaintyPct`** — bunch size varies 2–4× by store and
season, which `SPEC.md` already calls out.

Ambiguous cases exist (carrots, garlic, mushrooms). **Use judgement and list every call you
made in the report**, with the ones you were unsure about marked. A documented judgement is
useful; a silent one is not.

## 2. Counting must not break the weight relationship — this is the important part

Potatoes are bought as a 5 lb bag and cooked as "two potatoes". Both must work:

- every count-default produce item needs an accurate **`gramsPerCount`** so
  `5 lb → ~13 potatoes` and `2 potatoes → ~340 g` both resolve
- **cite the source** for each weight (USDA FoodData Central medium sizes preferred). Do not
  invent plausible-looking numbers — a wrong gramsPerCount silently corrupts inventory in both
  directions
- give genuinely variable items an honest `uncertaintyPct` rather than false precision
- keep a mass form available on these ingredients so a receipt line in pounds still resolves

## 3. Existing installs

Changing `defaultFormId` on a seeded ingredient can strand pantry rows that reference the old
form. Bump the seed version and make sure an upgrading device keeps its items — the locations
migration (`migrateLocationsTree`) is the pattern to follow.

**If a migration is needed, say so clearly in the report** — the app-side migration is a
separate track and needs to know.

Tests to add:

- a recipe line of "2 potatoes" resolves against a pantry holding potatoes bought by weight
- bunch-based items carry a high uncertainty rather than a precise fiction
