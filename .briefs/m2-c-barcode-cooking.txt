## Part 1 — Barcode

Scan a UPC/EAN and resolve it to a product, primarily for **put-away** (scanning items as you
unpack them) and as a fallback when a receipt line is unreadable.

Use `@capacitor/barcode-scanner` (or the ML Kit equivalent) on native; on web fall back to
`BarcodeDetector` where available and degrade gracefully where it is not.

### Open Food Facts — read this carefully

SPEC corrected an earlier over-simplification. OFF is **ODbL share-alike** with attribution,
**15 product reads/min/IP**, and requires a **custom User-Agent**. It is not "free, no strings".

Therefore:
- Set a descriptive User-Agent identifying this app.
- Respect the rate limit — client-side throttle, and degrade politely rather than hammering.
- **Tag every OFF-derived row as OFF-sourced and keep it segregated** from our canonical seed
  data. Do not merge OFF fields into canonical ingredient rows. Share-alike can attach to a
  derived database, and mixing makes that impossible to unwind later.
- Ship attribution in-app (a credit line in settings/about).
- Cache lookups locally so a repeat scan costs no request.

Resolution flow: barcode → OFF product (name, brand, quantity) → `matchIngredient` against our
catalog → user confirms → `purchase` txn. Unknown barcodes offer manual entry and remember the
mapping for next time.

## Part 2 — Cooking mode

A focused, hands-busy view for actually cooking a recipe. This is a kitchen, not a desk.

- **Screen stays awake** (`@capacitor/keep-awake`). Nothing is more annoying than a screen
  sleeping with wet hands.
- **Large type, high contrast, one step at a time**, big next/back targets. Assume the phone
  is propped on a counter at arm's length.
- **Step timers** — a step with a duration gets a start/pause timer with a notification when
  it finishes. Multiple concurrent timers must be possible; cooking is parallel.
- **Ingredient checklist** for the current step, scaled to the chosen servings.
- **NO ADS in cooking mode** — both AdMob policy (ads during focused tasks) and basic decency.
  The `AdSlot` component must not appear on this route.
- Exit leads into the existing cook → deduct preview flow; do not duplicate that logic.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; keep transitions minimal.
