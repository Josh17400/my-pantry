## The scope correction

The prior agent was building a **local SQLite database for web** and lost a fight with it
("Web fails on Drizzle's sync select path"). **Web SQLite is cut from the product entirely.**

Reason: `expo-sqlite` web support is officially alpha and needs `Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy`
and `Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy` headers for `SharedArrayBuffer`. Those headers are
**origin-wide** and break cross-origin popups and embeds — Google sign-in, AdSense, Stripe.js.
Even a working implementation would not have shipped.

| Target | Data layer |
|---|---|
| iOS / Android | `expo-sqlite` + Drizzle, offline-first. **The real app.** |
| Web | **Supabase-direct + service-worker cache. NO local database.** Online companion. |
