# BRIEF: Clamp the Remove wheel to what is actually on hand

## Context

**The Good Pantry.** Owner request:

> *"When switching to Remove under Adjust it should max out at the maximum possible you could
> remove. If I have 1.824 lb of chicken, go to Adjust, slide to Remove — the counting wheel
> should max out at 1.824 lb, so people don't give themselves a negative amount. If I dialled
> -2 lb I'd have -2.822 oz of chicken, which doesn't make sense."*

Correct. Dialling past the on-hand amount is a thumb slip, not a real event.

## Scope

`apps/web/src/ui/**` (the picker), `apps/web/src/features/pantry/**` (Adjust / Waste /
Mark used up sheets), `apps/web/scripts/verify-chrome.mjs`.

**Do NOT touch** `packages/core/**` (279 tests, read-only), `supabase/**`, `native/**`,
**and specifically do not touch `apps/web/src/features/recipes/**` or the cook flow** — see
the boundary below.

## 1. Clamp on removal

When the direction wheel is **Remove** (and in **Waste** / **Mark used up**, which are always
removals), the quantity wheel's maximum is the item's current on-hand amount, expressed in the
currently selected unit.

- 1.824 lb on hand → the lb wheel stops at 1.824 (or the nearest step at or below it).
- Switch the unit wheel to oz → the max becomes ≈29.2 oz. **Re-clamp on every unit change**,
  converting through `@larder/core`'s `convert()`.
- Switching **Add → Remove** while a larger value is selected must pull the selection down to
  the new maximum, not leave an out-of-range value sitting in the wheel.
- Switching **Remove → Add** lifts the cap again.
- An item at zero: the Remove path should be unavailable or clearly disabled, not a wheel that
  only offers zero.

Make the cap legible rather than mysterious — the wheel simply ending is confusing. Show the
available amount ("1.824 lb available") near the wheel.

## 2. When they hit the cap, point at Recount

Someone who genuinely used more than the record says does not have an input problem — they
have a **record** problem, and the answer is Recount, not a negative adjustment.

When the selection reaches the maximum, surface a quiet hint:

> *"That's everything recorded. Have more than we tracked? Use **Recount** instead."*

Link it so Recount opens from there. This turns a dead end into the correct next action.

## 3. The boundary — do NOT clamp the cook flow

`SPEC.md` is explicit that cooking more than the recorded amount **prompts** rather than
clamps: *"Going negative prompts 'still have some?' — that recovers reality better than either
alternative."*

That is a different situation. In the cook flow the shortfall is **discovered**, and it is
evidence the ledger has drifted — real signal worth surfacing. On the Adjust wheel the number
is **chosen**, and a value beyond what exists is just a mis-dial.

Leave `NegativeStockPrompt` and the cook flow exactly as they are. If you find yourself editing
them, you have misread this brief.

## Constraints

TypeScript `strict`, no `any`. Mobile-first. No new deps. Reuse the existing picker and sheet
primitives (`ui/layers.ts`, `Z_CLASS`). Create no git commits.
