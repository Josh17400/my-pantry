## Context

**The Good Pantry.** The owner cooked Black Bean Tacos on a real device and the cook flow
**dead-ended**. This is the product's core interaction, so it matters more than anything else

Read `SPEC.md` ("Cook planning"), `apps/web/src/routes/CookPage.tsx`, and

## 1. Prove the cook flow completes end to end — highest priority

The owner could not finish a cook. Until a test walks the whole path, we do not know it works.

Extend `scripts/verify-interactivity.mjs` (or add a sibling) that, against the demo profile:

1. opens a recipe → **Cook**
2. asserts the confirm control is **visible and clickable** — not merely present in the DOM.
   Use a real hit test: `elementFromPoint` at the button's centre must resolve to the button
   (or a descendant). A DOM-presence assertion is exactly what missed this bug.
3. clicks **Confirm cook**
4. asserts the success state appears (`Cook logged`, the `cookEventId`, the undo affordance)
5. navigates to the pantry and asserts **a specific ingredient's quantity actually decreased**
6. clicks **undo** and asserts the quantity is restored

Print the before/after numbers. This is the product thesis; it should be impossible to break
silently.

Also assert **no element is obscured by the tab bar** on every route that has a fixed or
sticky bottom control — the same hit test, applied generally. That class of bug will recur
otherwise.

## 2. Real substitution handling — owner's request, quoted

> *"If there is a substitution method you should probably allow the person to click that and
> then go through their pantry and click if they used something else as a substitution. And if
> they don't have it in the pantry they can click Other at the top of the screen and write in
> what it is — that way they can actually take out what they used."*

Today a substitution is a free-text note that deducts nothing, which is worse than useless:
the user believes they recorded it and the pantry silently stays wrong.

Build it properly. On any cook line, **Substitute** opens a picker that:

- **Searches the user's actual pantry**, ranked sensibly (same category first, then anything
  in stock). Show quantity and location so they can tell which item they mean.
- On selection: **deduct the substitute, not the original.** This is the whole point. Write
  the txn against the substitute's ingredient and form, converting units through
  `@larder/core`'s `convert()`. If conversion is not possible, ask for the amount rather than
  guessing.
- **"Other" at the top** for something not in the pantry: free text, recorded on the cook event
  for provenance, deducting nothing — and **say so plainly** in the UI ("noted, nothing
  deducted") so the user is not misled.
- The substitution must be visible on the confirm summary before committing.

## 3. Cook flow polish the owner ran into

- There must always be an obvious way **out** as well as forward. Back to the recipe, clearly
  placed, at every phase.
- After confirming, do not strand the user on the preview: show the result and offer
  **Done → recipe** and **Undo**.
- The step-through and the preview are currently disconnected. Finishing the guided steps
  should lead into the preview with continuity, not what feels like a different screen.
