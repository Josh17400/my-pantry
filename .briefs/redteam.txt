## The product in one sentence

A universal Expo app (iOS + Android + web) that keeps an accurate live food inventory by
subtracting ingredients when you cook a recipe and adding them when you photograph a
grocery receipt, with low-stock flags, generated grocery lists, community recipes, and a
paid-tier AI chef.

## Attack surfaces — probe every one of these specifically

1. **The unit model.** Three dimensions (mass/volume/count) normalized to g/ml/each, with
   ingredient density for cross-dimension conversion. Where does this break? Consider:
   "3 cloves garlic" vs a jar of minced garlic. "1 bunch cilantro." "1 can diced tomatoes"
   (drained weight vs total). "A pinch of salt." "2 slices bacon." Whole vs boneless chicken.
   Flour measured by scooping vs sifting (a real 20%+ variance). Is a 3-dimension model
   actually sufficient, or is a 4th needed?

2. **The ledger + projection model.** Append-only `PantryTxn`; `PantryItem.qtyBase` is a
   materialized projection. Attack: what happens at 50,000 transactions? Does the projection
   get rebuilt or incrementally maintained, and what happens when those disagree? What does
   a `recount` txn do to a projection that syncs from another device that did NOT see the
   recount? Is "union of an append-only log" actually conflict-free here, or does `recount`
   secretly break that property? (Consider this carefully — I believe it may.)

3. **Sync.** Two phones, same household, both offline. Phone A cooks spaghetti. Phone B
   cooks the same spaghetti (one person cooked, both logged it). Both reconnect. What is the
   pantry state and is it correct? Same question for: both scan the same receipt; A recounts
   flour to 500g while B cooks with 200g of it.

4. **The receipt pipeline.** What does it do with: a Costco receipt (item codes, no readable
   names, 40+ lines)? A receipt where the same item appears 3 times? A faded thermal receipt?
   A non-grocery receipt someone scans by accident? Items sold by weight with a price-per-lb?
   Store loyalty discounts as negative lines? A receipt in a language other than English?
   What is the worst-case user experience and how many taps is a bad scan?

5. **Par levels / low-stock.** Par level seeds from typical package size and then "learns"
   from median purchase quantity after 3+ purchases. Attack the cold-start (0-2 purchases),
   the seasonal buyer (turkey once a year), the bulk buyer, and the person who buys the same
   ingredient in two different sizes. Does "25% of par" produce useful alerts or alert
   fatigue? What is the actual notification strategy and will users turn it off?

6. **Ingredient matching.** A cascade of learned alias → global alias → normalized → fuzzy →
   LLM → ask user. Attack: how does a global alias table get poisoned by wrong user input?
   What is the fuzzy-match false-positive rate on things like "cream" vs "sour cream" vs
   "cream cheese" vs "heavy cream" — these are genuinely different ingredients with similar
   names, and a false match silently corrupts inventory. What is the guard?

7. **The universal-platform bet.** Expo + react-native-web, one codebase, three targets.
   Where does this actually hurt? Be specific about which planned dependencies have no web
   story and what the fallbacks cost.

8. **Scale and performance.** 500 pantry items, 2000 community recipes, 50k transactions,
   on a 4-year-old Android phone. What is slow? What query is missing an index?

9. **Retention and the real reason these apps fail.** Every pantry-tracking app on the
   market has the same graveyard pattern: users love it for two weeks, the inventory drifts
   out of sync with reality, and they abandon it. The spec's answer is receipt-in / recipe-out
   automation plus a "recount" flow. Is that actually sufficient? What is the honest failure
   mode, and what would meaningfully reduce it? THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT QUESTION IN THIS
   BRIEF — spend real effort here.

10. **Anything the spec does not mention at all.** Its own "gaps and oversights" section is
    an explicit claim of completeness. Falsify that claim.

## Also verify external facts (use web search, cite sources)

The spec was written by a model with a mid-2026 knowledge cutoff. Confirm or correct:

- Current stable Expo SDK version, and its React Native version.
- `expo-sqlite` web support status TODAY: does it work, what does it require (OPFS?
  COOP/COEP headers? a specific bundler config?), and what are people actually hitting?
  This is flagged in the spec as the single highest-risk item — get a real answer.
- Cheapest capable **vision** models on OpenRouter for receipt OCR → structured JSON, with
  current per-image cost. Does the spec's ~$0.002/receipt estimate hold?
- Open Food Facts API: current terms, rate limits, and whether commercial use in a
  monetized app is permitted.
- RevenueCat web billing support, and whether it is worth using over Stripe direct on web.
- NativeWind v4 current status with the current Expo SDK — any known breakage.
- AdMob policy specifics for banner placement in apps with full-screen "cooking mode" views.

Flag anything in the spec that is factually stale or wrong. Cite your sources.
