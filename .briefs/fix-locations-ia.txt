# BRIEF: Restructure locations — Freezer in, Around the House folded into Pantry

## Context

**The Good Pantry.** Owner request, from walking the app:

> *"Replace Favorites with freezer items, and then Around the House stuff should just be
> pantry stuff — and then let the drop-down go into the pantry stuff."*

The current tree is Fridge / Pantry / **Around the House** (which parents Spices, Tea & Coffee,
Baking, Household), plus a **Favorites** card on the home screen.

## Target

```
Fridge    (root)
Freezer   (root)   <- new; replaces the Favorites card
Pantry    (root)
  ├── Spices          <- reparented from Around the House
  ├── Tea & Coffee    <- reparented
  ├── Baking          <- reparented
  └── Household       <- reparented
```

**"Around the House" is removed entirely.** Its children become children of Pantry. The
location dropdown drills into Pantry's children.

## Scope

`apps/web/src/db/**` (seed, constants, migrations, dev driver, fixtures),
`apps/web/src/features/home/**`, `apps/web/src/features/pantry/**`.

**Do NOT touch** `packages/core/**` (279 tests, read-only), `supabase/**`, `native/**`,
`apps/web/src/features/recipes/**`.

## What to do

### 1. Seed and constants
- Add `freezer: 'loc-freezer'` to `DEFAULT_LOCATION_IDS`; pick a tint that reads as cold
  against the warm palette without breaking it (see `DESIGN.md` — everything is warm-shifted,
  so a cold tint must be chosen carefully, not grabbed from a generic blue).
- Remove `aroundHouse`. Reparent Spices / Tea & Coffee / Baking / Household to `loc-pantry`.
- Sort order: Fridge, Freezer, Pantry, then Pantry's children.

### 2. Migration — existing installs matter here
The owner's device already has rows referencing `loc-around-house`. A seed change alone leaves
them orphaned, and items would vanish from every view.

Write a migration that **reparents any location whose parent was `loc-around-house` to
`loc-pantry`, and moves any pantry item assigned directly to `loc-around-house` into
`loc-pantry`**, then removes the old location. Bump the seed version so it runs once.

Verify with a test that starts from the OLD shape and ends with no orphans and no lost items.

### 3. Home — At a Glance
- Cards become **Fridge · Freezer · Pantry**. Favorites is gone.
- Three cards in what was a 2×2 grid leaves a hole — handle it deliberately (a 3-up row, or
  2×2 with Pantry spanning). Do not leave a ragged gap.
- Each card still navigates to its filtered pantry view.
- The location dropdown lists Fridge, Freezer, Pantry **and Pantry's children**, indented or
  grouped so the hierarchy is legible. Selecting a child filters to it.

### 4. Pantry screen
Grouping follows the new tree. A parent's view should include its children's items (opening
Pantry shows Spices/Baking/etc. items too, grouped) — otherwise items become hard to find and
the sub-locations feel like separate silos.

### 5. Demo fixtures
Add believable freezer items (frozen peas, chicken breasts, berries, ice cream) so the Freezer
card is not empty when walking the app. Move any "around the house" fixture items to Pantry or
its children.

## Constraints

TypeScript `strict`, no `any`. Mobile-first. No new deps. Create no git commits.
